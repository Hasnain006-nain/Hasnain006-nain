| Work | What I built | Selected result | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Interpretable Financial Fraud Detection** | Leak-controlled evaluation of 12 models across three transaction datasets, with SHAP and McNemar testing | **0.9364 MCC**, **0.9904 PR-AUC** | Under review at *IEEE Access* |
| **PalmSightCare** | On-device cataract screening with a MobileNetV2 and custom-CNN benchmark | **99.69% accuracy**, **25.9 ms**, **10.4 MB** | Under review at *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control* |
| **TinyRAG** | Local RAG using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct on a 16 GB CPU laptop | **95.2% Recall@5**, **0.868 MRR** | Manuscript in preparation |
| **Day90 Guardian** | Multi-agent employee-risk system with six connected operators, semantic routing, and safe HR interventions | **2nd Place Asia** | Autopilot Asia Hackathon 2026 |
